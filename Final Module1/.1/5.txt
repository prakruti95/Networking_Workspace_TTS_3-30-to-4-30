# Day 4: GPUs and Cooling Systems

## Theory (30 Minutes)

### 1. What is a GPU?

A **GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)** is a specialized processor designed to handle graphics rendering and parallel computations.

Common uses:

* Gaming
* Video editing
* 3D rendering
* Artificial Intelligence (AI)
* Machine Learning
* Cryptocurrency mining

### Types of GPUs

#### Integrated GPU (iGPU)

Built into the CPU.

**Advantages:**

* Lower power consumption
* Lower cost
* Suitable for office work and media playback

**Examples:**

* Intel UHD Graphics
* AMD Radeon Graphics (integrated)

#### Dedicated (Discrete) GPU

A separate expansion card installed in a PCIe slot.

**Advantages:**

* Much higher performance
* Dedicated video memory (VRAM)
* Better for gaming and professional workloads

**Examples:**

* NVIDIA GeForce RTX series
* AMD Radeon RX series

---

### 2. GPU Components

A graphics card typically contains:

* GPU Chip (the processor)
* VRAM (Video Memory)
* Cooling System
* PCIe Connector
* Display Outputs (HDMI, DisplayPort)

### Common GPU Ports

* HDMI
* DisplayPort
* DVI (older)
* VGA (very old)

---

## 3. Cooling Systems

Computer components generate heat and must be cooled to operate safely.

Main components needing cooling:

* CPU
* GPU
* Power Supply
* Case interior

---

## 4. Air Cooling

Air cooling uses:

* Heat sink
* Cooling fan

### How It Works

1. Heat moves from the CPU/GPU into the heatsink.
2. The heatsink spreads the heat.
3. The fan blows air across the fins.
4. Heat is expelled from the case.

### Advantages

* Affordable
* Reliable
* Easy to install
* Low maintenance

### Disadvantages

* Can be noisy
* Less efficient for extreme workloads

---

## 5. Liquid Cooling

Liquid cooling uses:

* Water block
* Pump
* Tubes
* Radiator
* Fans

### How It Works

1. Coolant absorbs heat from the CPU.
2. Pump circulates coolant.
3. Radiator releases heat.
4. Coolant returns to the CPU block.

### Advantages

* Better cooling performance
* Quieter operation
* Attractive appearance

### Disadvantages

* More expensive
* More complex
* Potential leak risk (rare in quality systems)

---

## Air Cooling vs Liquid Cooling

| Feature             | Air Cooling | Liquid Cooling |
| ------------------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| Cost                | Lower       | Higher         |
| Installation        | Easier      | More complex   |
| Maintenance         | Minimal     | Moderate       |
| Cooling Performance | Good        | Excellent      |
| Noise               | Moderate    | Usually lower  |

## Practical (30 Minutes)

### Activity 1: Identify the GPU

Open the PC case and locate:

✅ Graphics card installed in the PCIe x16 slot

Look for:

* Large expansion card
* One or more cooling fans
* HDMI/DisplayPort outputs
* PCIe power connectors (6-pin, 8-pin, or 12VHPWR)

### Questions

1. Is the GPU integrated or dedicated?
2. How many fans does it have?
3. Which display outputs are available?

---

## Activity 2: Identify the CPU Cooling System

Locate the cooler directly above the CPU.

### Air Cooler Indicators

* Large metal heatsink
* One or two fans
* No tubes

### Liquid Cooler Indicators

* Tubes connected to CPU block
* Radiator mounted in case
* Radiator fans

---

## Activity 3: Identify Case Airflow

Find:

* Front intake fans
* Rear exhaust fan
* Top exhaust fans (if present)

### Typical Airflow Pattern

```
Front Fans  --->  Cool Air In

CPU/GPU Cooling

Rear/Top Fans ---> Hot Air Out
```

Good airflow helps:

* Lower temperatures
* Better performance
* Longer component lifespan

## Quick Quiz

1. What does GPU stand for?
2. Which slot is typically used for a graphics card?
3. What is VRAM used for?
4. Which cooling method uses a radiator and pump?
5. What is the main purpose of case fans?

### Answers

1. Graphics Processing Unit
2. PCIe x16 slot
3. Storing graphics-related data and textures
4. Liquid cooling
5. Moving air through the case to remove heat
