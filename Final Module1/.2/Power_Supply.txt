# Day 1: Overview of Peripherals

## Theory Content (1 Hour)

### Introduction to Peripherals

Peripherals are external devices connected to a computer to increase its functionality. These devices help users enter data, receive output, communicate, and store information. Peripherals are generally classified as input devices, output devices, and communication devices.

### Printer

A printer is an output device used to produce hard copies of documents, images, and reports on paper. Printers are commonly used in homes, schools, offices, and businesses.

#### Types of Printers

**Inkjet Printer**
An inkjet printer uses liquid ink sprayed through tiny nozzles onto paper. It is suitable for printing color documents and photographs.

**Laser Printer**
A laser printer uses toner powder and laser technology to create printed output. It is faster and more economical for high-volume printing.

#### Uses of Printers

Printing documents and reports

Printing photographs

Generating invoices and receipts

Producing educational materials

#### Printer Connection Interfaces

USB connection

Wi-Fi connection

Ethernet network connection

Bluetooth connection in selected models

### Scanner

A scanner is an input device that converts physical documents, photographs, or images into digital format. The scanned data can be stored, edited, or shared electronically.

#### Types of Scanners

**Flatbed Scanner**
A document is placed on a glass surface for scanning. It is commonly used for photographs and important documents.

**Sheet-fed Scanner**
Documents are automatically fed into the scanner, making it suitable for scanning multiple pages.

#### Uses of Scanners

Digitizing documents

Preserving old photographs

Creating electronic records

Converting printed text into editable text through OCR technology

#### Scanner Connection Interfaces

USB connection

Wi-Fi connection

Ethernet connection

### Webcam

A webcam is a digital camera connected to a computer for capturing live video and images. It is widely used for communication and online collaboration.

#### Types of Webcams

**Built-in Webcam**
Integrated into laptops and some monitors.

**External Webcam**
Connected separately through a USB port and often provides better video quality.

#### Uses of Webcams

Video conferencing

Online teaching and learning

Live streaming

Video recording

Capturing photographs

#### Webcam Connection Interfaces

USB connection

Wi-Fi connection

Bluetooth connection in some devices

### Common Connection Interfaces

**USB (Universal Serial Bus)**
The most commonly used wired interface for connecting peripherals.

**Wi-Fi**
Allows wireless communication between peripherals and computers.

**Ethernet**
Provides a stable wired network connection.

**Bluetooth**
A short-range wireless technology used for connecting compatible devices.

### Conclusion

Printers, scanners, and webcams are important peripherals that enhance computer functionality. Understanding their purpose, types, and connection methods helps users effectively operate and maintain computer systems.

# Practical Activity for Day 1 (30–40 Minutes)

## Activity 1: Peripheral Identification

### Objective

To identify different computer peripherals and their connection interfaces.

### Materials Required

Desktop computer or laptop

Printer

Scanner

Webcam

USB cable

Ethernet cable

Show each peripheral device to students.

# Day 2: Power Supply Systems

## Theory Content (30 Minutes)

### Introduction to Power Supply Systems

Every computer requires a stable power source for proper operation. Power supply systems ensure that computer components receive the correct voltage and remain protected from power disturbances.

### SMPS (Switched Mode Power Supply)

SMPS is an electronic device that converts alternating current supplied from the power outlet into direct current required by computer components.

### Functions of SMPS

Converts AC power into DC power

Supplies power to the motherboard

Provides power to storage devices

Supports cooling fans and expansion cards

Maintains stable voltage levels

### Advantages of SMPS

High efficiency

Compact size

Low heat generation

Reliable operation

### Main SMPS Connectors

#### ATX Power Connector

Provides power to the motherboard.

#### CPU Power Connector

Supplies power directly to the processor.

#### SATA Power Connector

Used for SSDs, HDDs, and optical drives.

#### Molex Connector

Used for older storage devices and accessories.

#### PCI Express Connector

Used for graphics cards.

### UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)

A UPS is a backup power device that provides electricity during power outages and protects systems from voltage fluctuations.

### Functions of UPS

Provides temporary backup power

Protects hardware from power surges

Prevents sudden shutdowns

Allows safe saving of work during power failure

### Types of UPS

**Offline UPS**
Provides backup power only when the main supply fails.

**Line Interactive UPS**
Regulates voltage fluctuations and provides backup power.

**Online UPS**
Continuously supplies power through its internal system and provides maximum protection.

### Advantages of UPS

Protects data

Improves system reliability

Prevents hardware damage

Supports uninterrupted work

### Conclusion

SMPS and UPS are essential components of a computer power system. SMPS supplies regulated power to internal components, while UPS provides backup power and protection against electrical disturbances.

# Practical Activity for Day 2 (30–40 Minutes)

## Activity 1: Identification of Power Cables and Connectors

### Objective

To identify various power connectors used inside a computer.

### Materials Required

Desktop CPU cabinet

SMPS unit

UPS

Power cables

### Procedure

Switch off the computer.

Open the CPU cabinet.

Locate the SMPS unit.

Identify the ATX connector.

Identify the CPU power connector.

Identify SATA power connectors.

Identify Molex connectors.

Identify PCI Express connectors if available.

Observe the UPS and its input and output connections.

Record observations.

### Observation Table

| Connector Name        | Connected Component | Purpose              |
| --------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------- |
| ATX Connector         | Motherboard         | Main Power Supply    |
| CPU Connector         | Processor           | CPU Power            |
| SATA Connector        | SSD/HDD             | Storage Device Power |
| Molex Connector       | Legacy Devices      | Peripheral Power     |
| PCI Express Connector | Graphics Card       | GPU Power            |

---

## Activity 2: UPS Demonstration

### Objective

To understand UPS operation during power failure.

### Procedure

Connect a computer to a UPS.

Turn on the computer.

Disconnect the main power supply to the UPS.

Observe that the computer continues to operate.

Discuss the importance of backup power.

Reconnect the power supply.

### Learning Outcome

Students will understand the role of UPS in maintaining uninterrupted computer operation and protecting data during power failures.
